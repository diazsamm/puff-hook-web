## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the warmth, comfort, and tactile charm of handmade crochet with a modern, high-end boutique finish. The visual narrative balances artisanal intimacy with polished digital presentation. 

### Brand Personality & Core Principles
- **Tactile & Plush:** Interfaces feel soft, pillowy, and touchable, echoing the physical sensation of looped yarn, puff stitches, and natural cotton fiber.
- **Heartfelt & Artisanal:** Every component showcases handcrafted care without sacrificing clarity, speed, or precision.
- **Modern Boutique:** Avoiding dated, cluttered rustic tropes in favor of an airy, luminous editorial layout reminiscent of slow-living lifestyle publications.

### Aesthetic Movement
A considered hybrid of **Soft Neomorphic Tactility** and **Modern Editorial Craft**. The design relies on pillowy radiuses, ultra-diffused blush shadows, woven fiber motifs, and warm cream backdrops to evoke the calming, meditative rhythm of crocheting.

## Colors

The palette revolves around whipped cream bases, confectionary blush pinks, and deep spun-berry accents. It creates a nurturing, daylight-filled atmosphere that puts handmade yarn textures at the center.

### Color Mapping & Roles
- **Base Canvas (`#FFFDF9`):** Pure warm alabaster cream. Provides warmth without the yellow heaviness of vintage parchment.
- **Surface Elevation (`#FDF8F5`):** Subtle warm ecru for card surfaces, flyouts, and modal containers.
- **Primary Accent (`#EC4899`):** Vibrant peony pink used for primary callouts, active state indicators, and focal interactive elements.
- **Secondary Accent (`#F472B6`):** Soft spun-sugar rose used for interactive surface states, secondary action buttons, and active tabs.
- **Tertiary Accent (`#BE185D`):** Rich cranberry/plum used for strong contrast elements, high-emphasis text links, and prominent badges.
- **Soft Veil Tint (`#FCE7F3`):** Pale blush tinted overlay used for chip backgrounds, input fills, and highlight areas.
- **Neutral Foreground (`#4A3B32` & `#786565`):** Warm yarn taupe and spun chestnut replace cold digital grays, keeping contrast high while preserving organic warmth.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the literary grace of **Playfair Display** with the friendly geometry of **Plus Jakarta Sans**. 

- **Display & Headlines:** Playfair Display introduces editorial warmth, reminiscent of boutique craft anthologies and pattern lookbooks. Italic styles are reserved for expressive secondary headers, product names, or pull-quotes to introduce a relaxed human signature.
- **Body & Captions:** Plus Jakarta Sans ensures pattern notes, material specifications, and shopping steps remain effortless to scan on all screen resolutions.
- **Rhythm & Numbers:** Tabular lining numbers in Plus Jakarta Sans are used for price displays, stitch counts, and dimensional measurements to ensure alignment in specifications.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an intentional, spacious cadence that gives high-resolution photography of looped yarn and physical crafts room to breathe.

### Grid & Responsiveness
- **Desktop (12 Columns):** 1200px max container width, 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters, 48px (`3rem`) screen margins. Employs asymmetric offsets for lookbooks and gallery showcases.
- **Tablet (8 Columns):** Fluid container width, 20px gutters, 32px margins. Two-column product grids reflow smoothly.
- **Mobile (4 Columns):** Fluid width, 16px (`1rem`) gutters, 20px (`1.25rem`) margins. Content blocks stack vertically, while chip filters and product swatches scroll horizontally with edge-snapping.

### Spacing Philosophy
White space is treated as an active design asset, mirroring the intentional pauses in craft patterns. Sections utilize wide vertical padding (`2.5rem` to `4rem`) to avoid visual density.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses plush, layered surfaces rather than stark, sharp drop-shadows.

### Shadow Characteristics
- **Tonal Glow Shadows:** Shadows are warm and diffused, tinted with a low-opacity berry blush (`rgba(190, 24, 93, 0.08)`) instead of black or cold slate.
- **Pillow Depth (Cards & Containers):** Dual-layer shadow: a soft ambient dispersion (`0 12px 32px -4px rgba(190, 24, 93, 0.06)`) paired with a tight bottom rim (`0 2px 6px rgba(120, 101, 101, 0.04)`).
- **Interactive Lift:** On hover, clickable surfaces experience a gentle upward translation (`-3px`) while the shadow expands (`0 18px 36px -4px rgba(236, 72, 153, 0.14)`), delivering a soft, buoyant lift.
- **Subtle Stitch Bordering:** Cards feature an inset or stroke of 1px solid `rgba(244, 114, 182, 0.15)` to mimic fine tailor stitching around soft forms.

## Shapes

The shape system draws inspiration directly from the curves of yarn balls, rounded crochet hooks, and puff stitches.

- **Base Components (0.5rem - 1rem):** Cards, input fields, and dialog wrappers feature comforting, rounded contours that eliminate sharp, harsh points.
- **Pills & Badges (Fully Rounded / 9999px):** Chips, tags, action buttons, and status indicators always adopt a pill geometry, echoing round yarn spools.
- **Stitched Dashes & Motifs:** Decorative dividing lines may feature gentle dashed styling (`border-style: dashed; stroke-dasharray: 6 6`) evoking thread and needlework.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Fully rounded pill silhouette. Solid `#EC4899` background with white text, bolstered by an ambient rose drop-shadow. Hover triggers a slight physical spring bounce (`cubic-bezier(0.34, 1.56, 0.64, 1)`) with a shift to `#BE185D`.
- **Secondary:** Cream background (`#FFFDF9`) with a 1.5px border of `#F472B6`, text in `#BE185D`. Hover brings an organic background transition to `#FCE7F3`.
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** No borders, warm text color with a soft underline that animates outward like unspooling yarn on hover.

### Chips & Badges
- Pill-shaped badges with compact vertical padding (`4px 12px`).
- Backgrounds use `#FCE7F3` with `#BE185D` typography. Used for crochet difficulty levels (e.g., "Beginner Friendly", "Puff Stitch"), yarn types (e.g., "Milk Cotton"), or availability tags ("Handmade to Order").

### Cards (Product & Pattern)
- Background surface of `#FDF8F5` finished with `1.5rem` rounded corners and faint rose border.
- Imagery includes an organic overflow or soft inner vignette.
- Metadata layout cleanly separates price, hook size indicator (e.g., "3.5mm Hook"), and title with generous internal padding (`space-lg`).

### Input Fields
- Generously rounded (`0.75rem`) with a plush `#FFFDF9` interior and inset shadow to feel recessed.
- Border shifts from delicate spun-rose (`#FCE7F3`) to an expressive focused ring in `#EC4899` (`2px outer glow`).
- Placeholder text is set in muted warm yarn taupe (`#786565`).

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Custom circular form controls. Checked states bloom with `#EC4899` featuring a rounded white checkmark or inner dot resembling a yarn knot.
- Interactive transitions feature a springy pop animation (`transform: scale(1.1)` before settling).

### Custom Domain Components
- **Yarn Colorway Swatch:** Interlinked circular swatches that highlight multiple yarn fibers per skein with an active crocheted-ring indicator.
- **Difficulty Meter:** A 5-point crochet hook icon indicator replacing sterile progress bars, showing the complexity level of each handmade pattern.
